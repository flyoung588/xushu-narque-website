01 / METRE

When Machines Can Work Continuously, How Might Economies Change?

The economic significance of AI extends beyond task automation. A deeper question is whether machine-operated systems can sustain meaningful economic activity under constraints of resources, knowledge, production, and capital—and how those capabilities might change production, organisation, and institutions.

02 / METRE

The Machine-Economy Era: A Historically Specific Concept

METRE distinguishes machines performing tasks from a historically distinct Machine-Economy Era. The latter describes a stage in which machine-economic systems capable of sustaining operations and evolving autonomously without any human participation have become the predominant form of macroeconomic organisation. Research examines the capabilities, constraints, and evidence such a transition would require.

Theory in Continuous Dialogue With Reality

METRE starts from consequential questions and refines its models through explicit definitions, formal reasoning, counterexamples, observed evidence, and competing explanations. Its research informs the economic context for XREM’s scientific questions and the changing constraints studied by AIOS.
