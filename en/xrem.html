01 / XREM

Discover New Factors and Functions of Production

We aim for machines to do more than answer established questions: to formulate scientific problems, explore unknown regularities, and discover knowledge, technologies, and capabilities that may expand the boundaries of production. Any claimed discovery must first withstand scientific scrutiny.

02 / XREM

Three Civilisation-Scale Scientific Missions

M1

Mastery of Energy: investigate unknown mechanisms and controllable capabilities in energy production, conversion, storage, and use.

02 ↗

Understanding Life: investigate foundational mechanisms of life, development, ageing, and regeneration.

03 ↗

03 / XREM

Exploration, Verification, and Reality in Dialogue

01 ↗

Open exploration

Start with observations, anomalies, and unknowns to generate questions, hypotheses, and search directions.

02 ↗

Digital verification

Use mathematics, computation, models, and qualified data to test scientific claims within clear scopes.

03 ↗

Physical verification

Use observations, experiments, instruments, and industrial reality to investigate claims that require real-world evidence.

04 / XREM

Build Reusable Scientific Verification Capital

XREM seeks not merely to generate more hypotheses, but to improve the reliability of deciding which hypotheses deserve further investigation. Qualified methods, known operating limits, failure evidence, and experimental feedback can make subsequent research more selective and effective.

05 / XREM

MFD | How Can AI Find the Right Scientific Frontier?

Mission–Frontier Decoupling (MFD) studies a foundational question: when existing knowledge and familiar verification paths pull research toward well-defined problems, how can AI continue selecting genuinely important scientific unknowns?
