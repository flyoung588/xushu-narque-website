MFD | Autonomous Scientific Frontier Selection

Mission–Frontier Decoupling (MFD) asks whether AI can identify consequential scientific unknowns rather than merely deepen established problems and familiar verification pathways. The research develops falsifiable comparisons and methods for choosing scientific frontiers.
