MACHINE ECONOMICS

If intelligence gets cheap,economics does not disappear.

The question becomes: what grows abundant, what remains scarce, and who controls the new bottlenecks?

CORE QUESTIONS

Current world model

  1. As intelligence gets cheaper, bottlenecks migrate rather than disappear.
  2. Verification capability may constrain the effective throughput of machine discovery.
  3. The value of physical scarcity depends on necessity, substitutability and control.

These are falsifiable propositions, not established empirical laws.
