GLOBAL RESEARCH / MSEC

Imperial College London | MSEC × ARIA

NARQUE is in research dialogue with Associate Professor Francesco Belardinelli of Imperial College London on MSEC (Minimum Sufficient Executable Constraints), exploring a possible joint research direction and an opportunity under ARIA’s Scaling Trust programme. The question is how machines might discover and create the enabling conditions required for economic interactions.

GLOBAL RESEARCH / MFD

Frontier AI Researchers | MFD

NARQUE has initiated scientific outreach to researchers associated with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind, while developing connections toward other frontier teams including xAI. The focus is MFD (Mission–Frontier Decoupling): enabling AI to move beyond solving known problems toward independently finding consequential scientific unknowns.

GLOBAL RESEARCH / STATION

Station | From Genome to Development

NARQUE has exchanged research ideas with the Station team and proposed a genome-to-development collaboration direction. The aim is to investigate how information encoded in the roughly three billion base pairs of the human genome interacts with the conditions of living systems—and where scientifically decisive leverage points may lie.

Collaboration Begins With a Shared Scientific Question

We welcome researchers and institutions able to frame discriminating questions, contribute rigorous verification, provide real experimental capacity, or bring strong counterexamples from other fields. A collaboration can begin with a theoretical conversation, a reproducible experiment, or a sharply defined research proposal.
