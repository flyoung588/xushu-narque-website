WHY NOW

Intelligence scales. Reality has a different clock.

AI makes candidate generation and search cheaper. In open physical domains, binding constraints may migrate toward trusted verification, authoritative Reality, time, capacity, and rights. This is theoretical and falsifiable.

Code alone is not a trustworthy verification capability. It must state where it works, what evidence supports it, how large its errors are, and when it must answer “unknown”.

Technical language

A Capability Card becomes production-trusted only after its validity boundary, qualification evidence, errors and abstention conditions are explicit.

XREM

Search the unknown. Let Reality judge.

XREM is not a prompt for more ideas. It turns a human-frozen real problem into machine Search, traceable candidates, digital verification, selective Reality and reusable learning.

Real problem→Searchable and judgeable Local Game→Machine Search→Traceable Candidate→Existing verification first→Reality only for unresolved questions→Qualified evidence may become reusable capability→Learning returns to Mission
